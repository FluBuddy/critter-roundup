Critter Roundup — Privacy Policy

Developer: FluBuddy Games  •  Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Critter Roundup ("the Game") handles information when you play on Android, Web, or iOS PWA. We respect your privacy and collect only the minimal data needed to run optional features such as cloud saves, friend trading, and rewarded advertisements.

1. Local Save Data

Your game progress (caught critters, coins, Sanctuary rooms, settings, and daily quest progress) is stored locally on your device. You can play offline without creating an account.

2. Optional Cloud Accounts & Trading

If you choose to create an optional account in-game to back up your save across devices or trade critters with friends, we store your chosen username, authentication credentials (handled securely via Firebase Authentication), and your game save data in Google Firebase. We do not sell or share your account information with third-party marketers.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android version of Critter Roundup uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads (for example, extra Daily Wheel spins or bonus nets). Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate location, and ad interaction data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Local Device Notifications

If you enable Phone Notifications in Settings, Critter Roundup schedules local reminders on your device (such as Daily Wheel spin resets, Egg Hatchery timers, and Sanctuary reminders). These reminders are scheduled locally on your device and do not send personal data to external servers. You can turn them off anytime in Settings → Phone Notifications.

5. Children's Privacy

Critter Roundup is designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information (such as real names, home addresses, or phone numbers) from children under 13.

6. Deleting Your Data

  • Local Save Data: Open Settings → Wipe Save Data in the Game, or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud Account Data: You may log out anytime in Settings, or request deletion of your optional cloud save by contacting the developer via the GitHub repository issues page at github.com/FluBuddy/critter-roundup.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as new features are added to Critter Roundup. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
